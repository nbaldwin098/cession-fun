Fees

Published trade fee is 0.95%. No hidden spread labels. A portion is intended to be paid back to customers on a daily schedule when that system is live. Statements are not tax advice.

Optional buffer

You may enable a buffer on sends and trades. While buffered, you can cancel before release. The buffer is not a guarantee after an action is fully released on-chain. Pure mainnet transfers without a buffer cannot be reversed.

On-chain sends

SOL sent to a token mint cannot be reversed. Cession blocks those destinations in our send box when classification is available.

Perpetuals

Perpetuals are not offered to persons in the United States.

Privacy
