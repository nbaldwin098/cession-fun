Legal & disclosures

Cession (cession.us) provides software interfaces for non-custodial crypto activity and, when partners are live, banking-as-a-service surfaces. We do not hold customer crypto or bank balances.

Non-custodial model

You connect your own wallet (Phantom, MetaMask, or Trust). Balances shown are read from the blockchain or from licensed partners. Cession cannot move funds without your signature or a partner execution you authorize.

Banking (BaaS)

Deposit accounts, cards, ACH, and cashback are provided by a licensed banking partner when connected. Cession does not itself take deposits. KYC is performed by the partner. Until partner keys are live, banking screens show zero balances only.

Crypto as a service (CaaS)

Buy and sell flows are routed through partner quote/order APIs. Settlement goes to your connected wallet or, for fiat out, to your partner banking rails\u2014not to Cession.

Xchange / bonding curve / Fuse

Token launches and trades on the bonding curve are separate entertainment/product surfaces. Fuse is an optional agent on coin create. Always verify on-chain. Tokens can go to zero.

Synthetic leverage / exposure

Any leveraged \u201crights\u201d product is not available to persons located in the United States. Where offered outside the US, it is synthetic exposure (rights), not ownership of the underlying token, and is subject to geo and KYC controls.

Fees

Platform fees may be set to zero during early operation. No hidden spread labels. Give-back comes from real treasury only\u2014nothing is invented as spendable balance before funding.

Optional send buffer

You may enable a timed buffer on outbound actions so you can cancel before release. After release on-chain or to a partner, actions generally cannot be reversed.

Risk

Digital assets are volatile and may lose all value. Smart contracts can fail. Partner outages can delay banking or crypto rails. This is not investment, tax, or legal advice.

Privacy

We may store wallet addresses you connect, device/IP signals for rate limits and geo rules, and product activity needed for support and fraud prevention.

Contact

Support via in-app Copilot and published support channels on cession.us.
