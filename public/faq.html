FAQ

Does Cession hold my crypto?

No. You connect Phantom, MetaMask, or Trust. Balances are read from the chain. We do not custody funds.

Where does a buy settle?

Crypto buys settle to your active connected wallet via partner rails when live. We never invent balances.

What is Banking on Cession?

Banking is coming soon. Request access on the Banking tab. Accounts and cards will be issued by a licensed partner when we go live.

What is the send buffer?

An optional delay on outbound actions so you can cancel before release. Turn it on inside Send / Buy sheets.

What is Xchange?

Fair-launch bonding curve coins and optional Fuse agent. Separate from Banking and the main Exchange.

Is leverage available in the US?

No. Synthetic leverage / rights products are not offered to persons in the United States.

How does daily give-back work?

When treasury is funded, micro rewards are sent via our crypto provider to your connected wallet. Nothing is shown as spendable until it is real.

How do I contact support?

Use in-app Copilot, or email support from the footer. Include your wallet address (never seed phrases).
