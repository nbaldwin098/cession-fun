# Cession \u2014 Investor one-pager

**Entity:** Delaware \u00b7 **Product:** cession.us  
**Thesis:** Non-custodial money app \u2014 Banking-as-a-Service + Crypto-as-a-Service first; Xchange (bonding-curve / Fuse) as secondary entertainment.

## Problem
Retail crypto UX is fragmented (wallet + CEX + bank + meme launchers). Pump-style launches collapse to zero and erode trust. Users need transparent rails, not invented balances.

## Solution
- **BaaS:** Partner-issued accounts/cards/ACH/cashback; Cession never holds deposits.
- **CaaS:** Partner quotes/orders; settlement to user wallet or partner fiat rails.
- **Wallet hub:** Multi-wallet tracking, on-chain SOL + SPL + native reads only.
- **Xchange:** Fair-launch bonding curve; optional Fuse agent; synthetic leverage geo-restricted (no US).
- **Safety:** Optional timed send buffer; rate limits; CSP; no in-app custody wallets.

## Regulatory posture (lowest-cost path)
- Non-custodial crypto software + partner banking.
- US users: spot / standard swaps only.
- Leverage / perps: non-US only, geo + KYC.
- No invented balances \u2014 API/chain or $0.

## Business model
- Transparent platform fee on CaaS and curve trades (can start at 0%).
- Partner revenue share on banking rails.
- Protocol give-back from real treasury only.
